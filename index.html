Questions

Pricing FAQs

Do you charge commission on bookings?

No. GoTrucking is a subscription platform. When you book directly with a shipper or transporter, you keep 100% of the freight value. We never take a cut of your trip.

Is the Transporter Free plan really free forever?

Yes. Single-truck owners can search up to 10 loads a day, receive basic lane alerts and contact verified shippers at no cost, for as long as they like.

What payment methods do you accept?

UPI, net banking, all major credit and debit cards, and NEFT/RTGS for annual plans. GST invoices are issued automatically for every payment.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from your dashboard. Upgrades are pro-rated and take effect immediately.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes — annual plans save you two months compared to monthly billing. Contact sales for multi-year and enterprise agreements.

Home / About

Building the digital highway for Indian freight

Founded in 2018, GoTrucking exists to make road transport in India transparent, efficient and fair — for the shipper, the transporter and the driver.

Our story

From a WhatsApp group to 5 lakh trucks

GoTrucking started with a simple frustration: India moves 4.6 billion tonnes of freight a year, yet most of it is still booked over phone calls and WhatsApp groups. Trucks run empty on the return leg, shippers overpay, and drivers wait weeks for payment.

We built a platform that puts every load, every rate and every document in one place — verified, tracked and settled digitally. Seven years later, over 5 lakh trucks and 25,000 businesses move freight on GoTrucking every month.

  • Transparency first

    Every rate on the platform is benchmarked against real market data — no opaque pricing.

  • Driver dignity

    Faster settlements, digital PODs and app support in nine Indian languages.

  • Less empty running

    Every matched return load takes a truck off a deadhead route — better for margins and for the planet.
